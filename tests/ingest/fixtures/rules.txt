SECTION 2— GENERAL RULES AND REGULATIONS
2.8    Signs
          a.    Only one (1) "for sale" sign of not more than three (3) square feet is allowed on each
                Unit. Signs cannot be located between the sidewalk and street. Signs are only permitted
                in yards and may not be affixed to any part of a home.
