(805 ILCS 105/102.05) (from Ch. 32, par. 102.05)
    Sec. 102.05. Incorporators. One or more incorporators may organize a corporation under this Act. Each incorporator shall be either a corporation, domestic or foreign, whether not for profit or otherwise, or a natural person of the age of 18 years or more.
(Source: P.A. 84-1423.)
