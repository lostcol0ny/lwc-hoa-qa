(805 ILCS 105/113.20) (from Ch. 32, par. 113.20)
    Sec. 113.20. Effect of authority. Upon the filing of the application for authority by the Secretary of State, the corporation shall have the right to conduct affairs in this State for those purposes set forth in its application, subject, however, to the right of this State to revoke such right to conduct affairs in this State as provided in this Act.
(Source: P.A. 96-66, eff. 1-1-10.)
